# SmartTech VisionBeam 4K Portable Projector (SKU: ST-VIS-002)
## User Guide & Maintenance Manual

### Section 1: Product Specifications
- Display Technology: 0.47" DLP Laser Light Engine
- Resolution: Native 3840 x 2160 (4K UHD)
- Brightness: 2,000 ANSI Lumens
- Autofocus: Time-of-Flight (ToF) Sensor with stepper motor alignment
- Keystone Correction: 6-point automatic omnidirectional keystone

### Section 2: Autofocus and Lens Calibration
The VisionBeam 4K uses an optical stepper motor to adjust lens focal length automatically.
#### Autofocus Noise and Grinding Issues:
If the lens makes an audible grinding or buzzing noise during autofocus, or if the projected image fails to focus:
1. Turn off the projector and unplug the power cable for 60 seconds.
2. Inspect the front optical lens assembly for any foreign debris, dust, or physical obstructions.
3. Power the projector on while pressing and holding the Focus (+) button on the remote for 10 seconds to initiate Motor Zero-Position Calibration.
4. If the grinding sound persists, the optical gear teeth may have suffered mechanical slippage.
