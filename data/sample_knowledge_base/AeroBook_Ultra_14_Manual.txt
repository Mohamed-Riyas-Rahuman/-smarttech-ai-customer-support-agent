SmartTech AeroBook Ultra 14 Laptop (SKU: ST-CMP-003)
Hardware Guide & Display Troubleshooting

Section 1: Hardware Specifications
- Processor: Intel Core Ultra 7 155H
- Memory: 32GB LPDDR5X 7467MHz dual-channel
- Display: 14.0-inch 2.8K 120Hz OLED (100% DCI-P3)
- Ports: 2x Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C), 1x USB-A 3.2 Gen 2, 1x HDMI 2.1 TMDS, 3.5mm audio jack.

Section 2: External Display & Thunderbolt 4 Connectivity
Section 2.1: Multi-Monitor Support
The AeroBook Ultra 14 can drive up to two 4K displays at 60Hz simultaneously via Thunderbolt 4, or one 8K display at 60Hz.

Section 2.2: External Display Flickering or Blackouts
If an external 4K monitor connected via USB-C or HDMI experiences periodic 2-second blackouts or flickering:
1. Ensure the Thunderbolt cable is rated for 40Gbps (USB4 / TB4 certified).
2. Update the Intel Graphics driver to version 31.0.101.5333 or later from the SmartTech Support Portal.
3. In Windows Display Settings -> Advanced Display, verify that the refresh rate matches the monitor specification (e.g. set to 60.00Hz instead of Variable Refresh Rate).
4. Disable "Display Power Saving Technology (DPST)" in the Intel Graphics Command Center.

Section 3: Warranty Coverage
The AeroBook Ultra 14 carries a 12-month manufacturer limited warranty covering logic board, CPU, RAM, display panel, and keyboard. Battery capacity is warranted against falling below 80% of original design capacity within the first 12 months.
