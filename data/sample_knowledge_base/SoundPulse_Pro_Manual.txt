SmartTech SoundPulse Pro ANC Headphones (SKU: ST-AUD-001)
Official User Guide & Technical Troubleshooting Manual

Section 1: Overview and Specifications
The SmartTech SoundPulse Pro features 45mm custom bio-cellulose dynamic drivers, dual hybrid Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), 40 hours of continuous playback, and Bluetooth 5.4 with LDAC and aptX Adaptive codecs.

Section 2: Pairing and Connectivity
To pair your SoundPulse Pro for the first time:
1. Ensure the headphones are powered off.
2. Press and hold the Power button for 5 seconds until the status LED alternates between flashing blue and white.
3. On your host device (smartphone, computer), open Bluetooth settings and select "SoundPulse Pro".
4. The LED will illuminate solid blue for 3 seconds upon successful connection.

Section 3: Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) Modes
Cycle through ANC modes by pressing the ANC button on the left ear cup:
- Mode 1: Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation (maximum 42dB attenuation).
- Mode 2: Transparency / Ambient Aware Mode.
- Mode 3: Normal / Passive Isolation Mode.

Section 4: Troubleshooting and Firmware Updates
Section 4.1: Charging and Battery Health
If the battery does not charge, ensure you are using the included 5V/2A USB-C charging cable. Rapid orange flashing indicates battery temperature exceeds 45°C; allow headphones to cool to room temperature before resuming charging.

Section 4.2: Firmware Update Stalls and Rapid Red LED
If a firmware update via the SmartTech Companion App stalls (typically between 40% and 50%) and the LED begins flashing red rapidly, this indicates a Bluetooth packet timeout during flash staging.
Resolution Procedure (Hard Factory Reset):
1. Disconnect the headphones from any charging cable or audio wire.
2. Press and hold down BOTH the Power button and the ANC button simultaneously for 12 seconds.
3. Release both buttons when the LED flashes purple twice.
4. On your smartphone/tablet Bluetooth settings, delete / "Forget This Device".
5. Power on the headphones, re-pair via Bluetooth, and retry the update in the SmartTech Companion App.

Section 5: Manufacturer Warranty Coverage
The SoundPulse Pro is protected by a 24-month comprehensive manufacturer hardware warranty from the original date of purchase or registration. It covers driver failures, battery degradation below 70% capacity, Bluetooth chip faults, and headband hinge defects. Accidental water immersion and physical breakage are excluded from standard coverage.
