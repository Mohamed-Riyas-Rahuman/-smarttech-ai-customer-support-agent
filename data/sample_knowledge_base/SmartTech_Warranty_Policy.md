# SmartTech Global Customer Service & Warranty Policies

## Section 1: Standard Warranty Terms
SmartTech warrants all brand hardware products against defects in materials and workmanship under normal consumer use.
- Audio Products (SoundPulse series): 24 Months
- Display & Projector Hardware: 12 Months
- Computing & Laptops: 12 Months
- Smart Home Hubs & Sensors: 12 Months
- Chargers & Accessories: 18 Months

## Section 2: Exclusions from Coverage
The following conditions immediately void or exclude warranty coverage:
1. Accidental drops, physical impact cracks, or liquid submersion (unless product is explicitly rated IPX8 waterproof).
2. Unauthorized disassembly, third-party component modification, or non-certified repair attempts.
3. Normal cosmetic wear and tear, including scratches, dents, and surface coating degradation.
4. Consumables (such as standard alkaline batteries or thermal pads) after 90 days.

## Section 3: Return Window and Refund Authority
- SmartTech offers a 30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee from date of delivery.
- Returned products must be in original packaging with all included accessories.
- Tier 1 Support Agents do NOT possess authority to issue cash refunds or chargeback approvals without Support Manager or Finance sign-off.
- Lost or stolen transit packages marked "delivered" require carrier investigation and escalation to Support Manager.
